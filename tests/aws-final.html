How this works

This is a full CLF-C02 mock exam. Like the real exam it has 65 questions in 90 minutes; 50 scored plus 15 unscored pretest items you can’t identify, and reports a scaled score out of 1000 with 700 to pass (this mock weights all 50 scored questions equally, so 35 correct = 700). Crucially, the 50 scored questions are distributed across the four official domains by their real weightings: Cloud Concepts 24% (12 questions), Security & Compliance 30% (15), Cloud Technology & Services 34% (17) and Billing, Pricing & Support 12% (6). Questions are drawn at random from a larger bank and reshuffled each attempt. Multiple-response questions must be fully correct. Flag and revisit questions before submitting; at the end you get a pass/fail result, a per-domain breakdown and full explanations.

This is a practice mock, not the real AWS exam. Scores here are an approximation to help you gauge readiness.
