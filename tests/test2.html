How this works

This mock mirrors the official SC-900: a scaled score out of 1000 with a passing bar of 700. Microsoft uses difficulty-weighted scaling; this practice test approximates it by weighting all 50 questions equally (20 points each), so 35 correct = 700. Questions are drawn at random from a larger bank and reshuffled each attempt. Multiple-response questions must be fully correct to earn points. You can flag questions and revisit them before submitting. At the end you’ll get a pass/fail result, a per-domain breakdown, and full explanations.
